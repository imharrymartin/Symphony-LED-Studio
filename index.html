Brand Setup Quick-Reference

Ensure your smart lights are configured for local LAN communication:

Magic Home / Flux LED: Connects directly over TCP port 5577. Works automatically on local WiFi without extra app configuration.
Govee Lights: Open the Govee Home smartphone app → select device → tap the top-right Settings gear → switch LAN Control to ON.
Yeelight Smart Bulbs: Open the Yeelight app → Device → Settings icon → enable LAN Control.
WLED Lightstrips: Fully open by default. Enter the IP of your ESP8266/ESP32 WLED device.
OpenRGB: Launch OpenRGB on your PC → go to the 'SDK Server' tab → click 'Start Server' (runs on port 6742).
Arduino / FastLED: Plug in your microcontroller via USB and upload an Adalight sketch or standard serial RGB receiver. Enter the COM port (e.g. COM3).
